Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our Banjara language preservation platform and services.

What is the purpose of this website?

+

Our platform is dedicated to preserving and promoting the Banjara language through AI-powered translation tools, learning resources, and community engagement initiatives.

Who can use this website?

+

Anyone interested in learning, preserving, or using the Banjara language can use our platform. It's designed for students, educators, cultural enthusiasts, and members of the Banjara community.

What languages are supported for translation?

+

Currently, we support translation between English and Banjara language. Our platform provides both text-to-text and speech-to-text translation services with high accuracy.

Are there learning resources available?

+

Yes! We provide comprehensive learning resources including interactive lessons, a digital dictionary, cultural primers, and community resources to help you learn Banjara language effectively.

How can I get technical support?

+

You can reach our technical support team at aadi.vaani@tribal.gov.in or through the contact form on our website. We're here to help you with any questions or issues you may encounter.
