Continuous Speech Translator

Speak naturally and get real-time translations. Your speech is processed continuously for seamless conversation. Toggle visibility of your original speech as needed.

Select languages and press microphone to start

How to use:

  1. Select source and target languages
  2. Click the microphone to start speaking
  3. Your translation will appear in real-time
  4. Click "Show Speech" to view your original words
  5. Click the microphone again to stop
